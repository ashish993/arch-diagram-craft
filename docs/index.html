01 / Read

Start from what’s known

Separate source-backed facts from inferences and open questions. Don’t fill gaps with plausible-sounding technology or controls.

02 / Draw

Make relationships explicit

Choose the right view, group by meaningful boundaries, and give important connectors a source, destination, direction, and label.

03 / Inspect

Check the rendered result

Review legibility, overlaps, arrowheads, boundary crossings, and numbered flows in the diagram at its intended size.

A diagram should answer more than “what connects?”

  • 01Which source statement supports each important component and flow?
  • 02Where does data cross a trust boundary, and in which direction?
  • 03Can a reader follow every arrow to its intended endpoint?
  • 04Are assumptions and missing details visible instead of quietly invented?
