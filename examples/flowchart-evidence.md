# Appointment rescheduling user flow: example evidence

**Status:** fictional user-facing flowchart for product and business discussion.

## Scenario statements used

| ID | Scenario statement | Diagram representation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | A patient opens an existing appointment and chooses reschedule. | Start and user actions |
| F2 | The service checks whether alternative slots are available. | Availability decision |
| F3 | When options exist, the patient selects a slot and confirms the change. | Selection and confirmation path |
| F4 | When no options exist, the patient chooses to keep the existing appointment or contact the clinic. | No-availability branch |
| F5 | A successful change ends with an updated appointment confirmation. | Success outcome |

## Assumptions / open questions

- Cancellation, identity re-verification, appointment eligibility, fees, and notification preferences are outside the scenario.
- The branch choices are invented to make the example concrete. Confirm them with product and clinic owners before reuse.
