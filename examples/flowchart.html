Flowchart: reschedule an appointment

A product and business-facing flow that shows what a patient does, where availability changes the route, and how a user can recover without losing an existing appointment.

Fictional teaching example. The rescheduling policy and outcomes are fictional. Eligibility, fees, identity checks, notification preferences, and cancellation behavior are outside this simplified example.

On narrow screens, scroll sideways or focus the named diagram and use the arrow keys.

Reading this view

A user flow follows one persona through screens and decisions. Its arrows show user progression and recovery choices, not API timing or internal service dependencies.

  • Yes and No labels are placed at the availability decision.
  • The no-slots path preserves the original appointment state.
  • Return-to-existing and contact-clinic outcomes are explicit; no policy is implied.
